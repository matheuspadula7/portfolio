Diálogos Estruturantes started as a magazine about taxation and is becoming a platform that connects the agribusiness, infrastructure and ports sectors, bringing together content, podcast, events and networking. The new brand and positioning were already defined. What was missing was a place to communicate the change while the full platform site wasn't ready yet.

The deadline was short. This is the path from brief to a live page in **3 days**.

## The challenge: tight deadline, new brand

The landing page had to be faithful to the new identity, attract the right audience and already reflect the ambition of the platform. At the same time, it couldn't turn into a big project: the full site was coming later.

With little time, the most important decision is **what to leave out**. The page had one job: present the new proposal and capture interest from the people who fit it.

## Step 1: wireframe before any code

I started by structuring the wireframe directly in Claude, through an interactive artifact. At this stage I defined the content hierarchy, the blocks and the reading flow, without worrying about visuals yet.

This sped up validation with the client right from the start. Adjusting a structure idea is much faster than redoing a page that has already been designed and coded.

## Step 2: design and code together, in Framer

With the structure approved, I built the visual layout and the code in Framer, applying the new identity to every section: from the hero to the presentation of the three sectors (agribusiness, infrastructure and ports) and the platform's content formats.

Working on design and implementation in the same place removes the "hand the layout to a developer" step, which is usually where deadlines slip.

## Step 3: lead capture from day one

The form was coded to deliver contacts straight into the client's **RD Station**. That way, the list of interested people started to grow before the official launch of the full platform.

A landing page that doesn't capture contacts is just a poster. Having the form integrated from the start is what turned it into a working tool.

## The result

- **3 days** from structured wireframe to published page
- **Leads delivered straight into the client's RD Station**
- **Architecture ready** for the three sectors: agribusiness, infrastructure and ports

You can see the full project on the [case page](/dialogos).

## What I take from this project

A short deadline doesn't have to mean a weak page. It calls for clearer decisions: define the structure before the visuals, choose a single goal for the page and reduce the number of steps between the idea and publishing.
